# Learn Marketing Analytics with Phoebe

Marketing analytics for data teams: data analysts and data scientists who serve marketing, brand
and BD. Ten 45-minute sessions in two tracks, by Phoebe Fu.

- **Angles (6 sessions):** how marketers think and the decision behind every ask; owned and earned
  channels (search, content, organic social, creators, PR) and paid and lifecycle channels (paid
  search, paid social, email, affiliate, paid influencer, events), each read in four beats; acquisition
  cohorts by channel; CAC, payback and the next dollar; campaign lift and brand health.
- **Partnership (4 sessions):** what marketing, brand and BD are measured on; the ownership map and a
  request triage desk computed in the browser; an intake template and a service menu; pushback
  scripts that open with a deliverable and a date.

The running company, Kettle & Co, is constructed. Platform facts are read from the platforms' own
help pages and dated; contested claims are taught as contested.

Static HTML, no build step. Open `index.html`, or serve the folder with `python3 -m http.server`.
