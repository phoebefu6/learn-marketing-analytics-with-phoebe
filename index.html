Learn Marketing Analytics with Phoebe

Data analysts who serve marketing, brand and BD get two kinds of question wrong. The first kind is about the numbers: a follower count that decides nothing, an open rate inflated since 2021, a platform ROAS the platform grades itself. The second kind is about the job: "why won't data do our market research?" These ten sessions teach both. Six Angles sessions read every channel in the same four beats, what it is, what marketers watch, the vanity trap, and where data adds value, then cohorts, segment value and lift. Four Partnership sessions stand in marketing's shoes, tag every ask by the knowledge it needs, and draw the line the whole course argues for: market research and penetration strategy belong to marketing and BD, and data is consulted with a named deliverable and a date. The running company, Kettle & Co, is constructed.
